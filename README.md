# The Diet Coke Index
*(Code Name: FRIDGE CIG)*

**Real-time retail arbitrage for the 2:15 PM silver-can ritual.**

Nobody opens a browser at 2:15 PM on a Tuesday because they're simply "thirsty." If they were thirsty, they'd drink tap water. They're here because their eyes are vibrating from staring at timelines, and they need that crisp, 34-degree, aspartame-laced metallic snap that signals they will survive the afternoon. The internet calls it a **Fridge Cig** because that's what it is: a legal, zero-calorie, silver-can ritual.

The retail beverage market is wildly inefficient. You walk into the wrong bodega, and you're paying $3.49 for a lukewarm 20oz plastic bottle. Meanwhile, 400 yards away, a fountain Big Gulp is $1.09, and two blocks further, a grocery store is running a promotion where 24-packs cost $0.37 a can.

**The Diet Coke Index** treats a 12oz aluminum cylinder with the cold, ruthless financial precision of a Bloomberg Terminal, wrapped in high-gloss pop-art luxury.

## The Burn vs. The Stash

<img src="docs/images/terminal.png" alt="The Diet Coke Index desktop view showing The Burn and The Stash cards side by side">

One screen. Zero fluff. One tap for coordinates. Two immediate financial decisions:

- **THE BURN (Immediate Relief):** Finds the lowest unit-cost cold single-serve within walking distance right now. Shows distance, walking time, unit cost ($/fl oz), and deep-links directly to turn-by-turn directions.
- **THE STASH (Bulk Arbitrage):** The Wall Street breakdown for fridge-loading. Calculates your **Unit Cost per Can**, **Caffeine Yield ($/mg)**, and **Bodega Single Spread (-85%)**, including regional runner-up promotions.

Color and type hierarchy are immediate: massive athletic grotesque numbers (**Anton**) dominate the viewport, supported by high-contrast labels and tabular numerals (**Inter**) set against a brushed anodized aluminum surface.

## The Apex Predator: McDonald's 34°F Closed-Loop Schematic

<img src="docs/images/schematic.png" alt="McDonald's Gold Standard Closed-Loop Schematic Modal">

Whenever McDonald's is identified within striking distance, the index badges it as **The Gold Standard**. Tapping the schematic button surfaces an interactive technical blueprint explaining why McDonald's fountain Diet Coke hits differently:

1. **Insulated Stainless Steel Vessels:** Coca-Cola delivers syrup in insulated stainless steel bulk tanks rather than plastic bladder bags, preventing oxygenation and plastic leaching.
2. **34°F Recirculation Loop:** Water lines are insulated and pre-chilled right to the brink of freezing (34°F) so the liquid absorbs and holds maximum dissolved CO₂ volumes.
3. **Pre-Cooled Syrup Ratio:** Syrup enters the fountain head cold to match water temperature, preventing violent decarbonation and foaming during dispensing.
4. **Wide-Bore Straw Dynamics:** Calibrated with a larger cross-section to deliver effervescent carbonation simultaneously across the entire palate.

## Tactile Physical Condensation & Pull-Tab Audio Engine

The interface isn't flat glass; it feels like cold metal in your hand:

- **Condensation Droplets:** Interactive SVG droplets anchored to the surface with dual white specular highlights, refractive caustic glow, and drop shadows. Tapping any droplet triggers a synthesized bubble pop sound effect and wipes clean.
- **Pull-Tab Sound Engine:** When coordinates are locked or refreshed, a zero-latency Web Audio procedural synthesizer simulates the mechanical aluminum tab fracture and fizzy hiss.
- **Haptic Feedback:** Native mobile vibration triggers a crisp tactile pop on supported devices.

## 3-Tier Format Matrix & Upfront Outlay Transparency

In the supermarket retail market, 12-pack bundles often beat 24-packs on pure unit economics ($0.42/can vs $0.54/can), but require buying 5 cases at once. The Stash features:
- **12-PACK:** Best unit cost promo specials ($4.99/pk with $24.95 upfront requirement).
- **24-PACK:** Single-case low friction shelf option ($13.00 single case).
- **COSTCO 36-PK:** Wholesale club bulk flat benchmark ($17.49 club flat).
- **Interactive Routing:** Tapping any tier immediately updates driving directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps.
